# Case Study Template

## Structure

### 1. The Problem (2-3 paragraphs)
- What was the situation before?
- What pain was being felt?
- Why was it important to solve?

### 2. The Approach (3-5 paragraphs)
- Initial hypothesis
- What was tried first (and why it failed)
- Iterations and pivots
- Final approach chosen and why

### 3. The Solution (2-4 paragraphs)
- What was actually built
- Architecture decisions and trade-offs
- Key code/implementation details
- Include real metrics if available

### 4. Results (1-2 paragraphs)
- Before/after metrics
- User feedback or adoption data
- Unexpected benefits or side effects

### 5. What We'd Do Differently (1 paragraph)
- Honest assessment of the approach
- Known limitations
- Advice for others facing similar problems

## Formatting Rules
- Use real numbers and metrics where possible
- Include architecture diagrams
- Show before/after comparisons

## Length: 1500-3000 words
