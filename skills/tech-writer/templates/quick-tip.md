# Quick Tip Template

## Structure

### 1. The Insight (1-2 sentences)
- The single takeaway
- Why it matters

### 2. The Problem (2-3 sentences)
- What most people do wrong
- The mistake or misunderstanding

### 3. The Fix (1 code block + 2-3 sentences)
- Show the wrong way → the right way
- Explain why the right way works

### 4. The Context (1-2 sentences, optional)
- When this applies
- When it doesn't

## Formatting Rules
- Get to the point immediately
- One insight, one tip
- No preamble, no conclusion

## Length: 300-600 words
