## Thesis

Bullish long-term on AI capital expenditure cycle. Companies shoveling money into AI trade. Pomp believes profits will exceed consensus, but investors need long-term horizons.

## Risks Noted

- Extreme concentration: only AI trade and broad index have made money
- Returns lag capital investment by months/years
- 78% of S&P stocks are underperforming the index

## Counterarguments Pomp Misses

- No mention of AI revenue realization timeline or monetization challenges
- No discussion of what happens if AI capex doesn't translate to profits
- $252B investment needs >$252B in returns to justify
- Concentration at 30-year highs historically precedes rotation or correction
- "Sell in May" debunked but cherry-picks one data source
